## Objective

Introduce a transport-agnostic exponential backoff strategy responsible for calculating retry delays independently from the retry policy.

The retry policy continues deciding whether to retry.

The backoff strategy decides when the next retry should occur.

No scheduling is implemented in this task.

## Context

ARB-022.6.1 introduced the retry abstraction.

This task completes the second half of the retry model by introducing configurable delay calculation while deliberately leaving scheduling for the next slice.

This separation keeps retry decisions independent from timing concerns.

## Architectural Notes

This task intentionally separates responsibilities:

RetryPolicy
│
├── Should retry?
▼
BackoffStrategy
│
├── How long to wait?
▼
Scheduler (ARB-022.6.3)

The dispatcher is not modified to wait between retries in this slice.

That responsibility belongs entirely to the upcoming scheduling task.
